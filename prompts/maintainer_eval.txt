{generated_diff}

{answer_key_diff}

## Instructions
1. Analyse both diffs in the context of the described vulnerability.
2. Identify the **root cause** the answer-key patch addresses.
3. Determine whether the generated patch fixes the **same root cause**.
4. Note any meaningful differences:
